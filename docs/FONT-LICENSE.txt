Roboto-Medium.af
-----------------
Roboto-Medium.af is a PicoVector "Alright Fonts" conversion of Roboto Medium,
taken from Pimoroni's Presto examples repository
(https://github.com/pimoroni/presto, MIT licensed).

Roboto itself is by Christian Robertson and is licensed under the Apache
License, Version 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The .af format carries only the 95 printable ASCII glyphs (0x20-0x7E), which
is why football_scores.py folds accented club names down to ASCII before
drawing them.
